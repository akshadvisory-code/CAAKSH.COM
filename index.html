About Us

About AKSH & Co.

M/s. AKSH & Co. is a premier Chartered Accountancy firm headquartered in Bhilwara, Rajasthan, with branch operations in Jaipur, Indore, Mumbai, and Morena. Established in 2019 (reconstituted from Agrawal Abhishek Kapil & Co.), the firm is registered with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India.

Led by a core leadership team of experienced FCA partners and supported by 15+ specialised professionals, including Big-4 alumni, we deliver comprehensive financial and advisory solutions. Our core focus spans Project Finance, Debt Syndication, Large-Scale Infrastructure Advisory, Statutory & Internal Audits, Special Audits, and Corporate Assurance under IND-AS frameworks.
